A little about me

I’m drawn to
the physical side
of engineering.

I study biomedical engineering at Georgia Tech, with a bachelor’s degree expected in May 2027. I’m interested in medical-device development—especially the work of designing a part, building it, and finding out how it behaves.

My coursework puts me at the bench; my research brings in sensing, computation, and biomechanics. I also teach: first as a Statics TA, and now in Biotransport.
