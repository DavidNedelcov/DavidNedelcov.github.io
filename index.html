04 BMED 2310 | Semester-long, five-person team Foley catheter redesign Contributed across ideation, CAD, prototype fabrication, testing, calculations, design-history documentation, and presentation. View evidence 1 semesterfull design cycle 5-person teamcollaborative project Broad roleCAD through testing

Design challenge

Reduce urethral tissue trauma when a Foley catheter is pulled before clinical removal, with men over 70 identified as the primary user group.

Team process

  • Generated 25 concepts and screened them with Benefit-Effort and Pugh matrices.
  • Prototyped breakaway drainage and foldable retention concepts.
  • Used a 13 N removal-force threshold to guide iteration.

Results and scope

  • Breakaway prototypes detached at 3.2 N and 6.9 N.
  • Retention-cushion iterations measured 8.3 N to 12.3 N.
  • Results are from the team's shared design history file.
05 BMED 3410 | Individual semester project Femoral compression model Independently calculated material properties, built the model, configured the Fusion simulation, interpreted results, and wrote the report. View evidence Individualfull ownership 1 semesterbiomechanics project Fusion FEAmodel and analysis

Model and results

Built a 120 mm concentric cylinder with a 30 mm cortical diameter and 18 mm trabecular diameter, fixed at the base and loaded at 2500 N. The model produced 7.704 MPa maximum stress and 0.065 mm displacement.

Material inputs

  • Cortical Young's modulus: 16.6 GPa; yield strength: 116.9 MPa.
  • Trabecular Young's modulus: 0.93 GPa; yield strength: 6.1 MPa.
  • Minimum simulated safety factor: 1.234.

Limitations

  • Idealized cylindrical geometry and isotropic linear elasticity.
  • No bending or torsional loading modes.
  • Population and osteoporotic representation limited by source data.
