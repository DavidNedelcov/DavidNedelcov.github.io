01 BMED 2310 | Team device design Safer Foley catheter removal A design-history-file project addressing tissue injury from accidental post-discharge catheter removal through concept screening and prototype testing. View evidence

Design challenge

Reduce urethral tissue trauma when a Foley catheter is pulled before clinical removal, with men over 70 identified as the primary user group.

Process

  • Generated 25 concepts and screened them with Benefit-Effort and Pugh matrices.
  • Prototyped breakaway drainage and foldable retention concepts.
  • Used a 13 N removal-force threshold to guide iteration.

Results and scope

  • Breakaway prototypes detached at 3.2 N and 6.9 N.
  • Retention-cushion iterations measured 8.3 N to 12.3 N.
  • Team results are summarized from the shared design history file.

04 Medical-device risk analysis Fogarty catheter use-FMEA An instructions-for-use analysis connecting user actions to foreseeable errors, hazardous situations, harms, controls, and mitigation priorities. View evidence 6task steps 22risk rows 9unacceptable risks

Method

Decomposed the IFU into prepare, purge, inspect, place, inflate, and withdraw tasks, then scored failure modes by severity and likelihood.

Critical hazards

  • Air embolism from incomplete balloon-lumen purging.
  • Vessel perforation, dissection, or rupture during placement and inflation.
  • Catheter separation or injury from excessive withdrawal force.

Controls reviewed

  • Sterile packaging, inspection requirements, and balloon-volume limits.
  • Heparinized saline purging and explicit IFU warnings.
  • Contraindications for fibrotic, adherent, or calcified material.
05 BMED 3410 | Structural simulation Femoral compression model A simplified cortical and trabecular shaft model used to compare analytical material properties with static structural response under walking-scale load. View evidence 2500 Naxial load 7.704 MPamaximum stress 0.065 mmdisplacement

Model

Built a 120 mm concentric cylinder with a 30 mm cortical diameter and 18 mm trabecular diameter, fixed at the base and loaded at the top.

Material inputs

  • Cortical Young's modulus: 16.6 GPa; yield strength: 116.9 MPa.
  • Trabecular Young's modulus: 0.93 GPa; yield strength: 6.1 MPa.
  • Minimum simulated safety factor: 1.234.

Limitations

  • Idealized cylindrical geometry and isotropic linear elasticity.
  • No bending or torsional loading modes.
  • Population and osteoporotic representation limited by source data.
