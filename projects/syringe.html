01 / CAD practice

Build the assembly part by part.

I modeled the barrel, plunger, fluid volume, and needle of a 5 mL syringe in Fusion. This was an individual CAD assignment within BMED 2310, focused on representing component geometry and how the parts fit together.

02 / Scope

A focused modeling exercise.

The work developed my practice with multicomponent assemblies and communicating internal geometry. It complements the broader catheter projects, where CAD was one part of a longer design and testing process.
